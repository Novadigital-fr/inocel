How the numbers are built

01

Your energy demand

Power, daily hours and yearly days give the electrical energy your site consumes over a year.

02

The diesel it would take

A generator set burns roughly a quarter of a litre per kilowatt-hour at typical load.

03

The emissions avoided

Each litre of diesel releases 2.68 kg of CO₂. A GEN-Z unit emits none of it on site.

These are planning figures, not a commitment. Real consumption depends on your load profile, ambient conditions and how the unit is operated. Send us your site data and an engineer will model it properly.

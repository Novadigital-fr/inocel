(Zero-emission power)

INOCEL decarbonizes your operations

At INOCEL, we turn clean energy into zero-emission, plug-and-play power solutions for modern industries, including data centers, events, construction sites, and critical infrastructure.

Every unit ships as a self-contained, road-transportable system. Connect the fuel, connect the load, press start. There are no civil works to plan and no permits to obtain for on-site combustion, because there is no combustion.

Reliable power, delivered

Our zero-emission generators deliver reliable, modular, non-polluting power with no direct emissions, enabling off-grid operations, backup systems, and uninterrupted power supply while supporting your decarbonization goals.

The GEN-Z 300 accepts full load in seconds and runs in parallel with other units, so capacity follows your demand instead of dictating it.

See the specifications

Patented technology, 25 years in the making

At INOCEL, our patented technology delivers high performance and reliability, enabling clean, zero-emission, non-polluting power generation.

The stack is the result of more than twenty-five years of research conducted with the CEA, and it is manufactured at our Belfort gigafactory. It converts hydrogen into electricity with water and heat as the only outputs.
